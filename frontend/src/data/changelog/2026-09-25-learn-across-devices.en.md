Until now, ShadowLearn worked only in Chrome on one device. The lessons you built there could not follow you to your phone or another computer. That limit is gone.

Sign in with the same account on your devices, and pick up where you left off. Study a full lesson at your desk. Review saved words on your phone later. Your lessons and progress stay together.

**ShadowLearn is still free.** Open it on another device, sign in, and keep learning.
